***The end of our wait finally begins…***

> We’ve waited, we’ve hoped, we’ve pushed through the miles—
Through late-night calls, tired mornings, and stolen smiles.
But baby… that chapter’s ending. A new one’s getting its start.
It’s time we finally take the steps to close the distance in our hearts.


> San Diego babe, its calling your name,
Our home. Our future. Our everything.
No more counting weeks or wishing days would end,
I promised youll never have to feel alone again-

---

> L is for Living, like the life we’ve dreamed about,
Not just a glow from our screen to settle our doubt.
<br><br>
O is for Ours, like the place we’ll finally share,
A bed, a home, a family, id take you anywhere.
<br><br>
U is for United, the way we’re meant to be,
No borders, no oceans, nothing separating you from me.
<br><br>
I is for Infinite, like the love we’ve grown so strong,
It’s carried us this far and will do so our whole life long.
<br><br>
S is for San Diego, where your dreams will all come true,
Where the waves and cloudy skies all make room for me and you.
<br><br>
A is for Always, because that’s just what we’ll be—
From the very first moment, until you arrive, and for eternity.

---

> Louisa, my love…
It’s time.
Time to bring you here.
Time to close the miles.
Time to start the future that we dream of,
So everyday I can see you smile


No more being apart,
I told you your stuck with me.
